# Screenshots

Captured in demo mode from the production build, with a session of 20 fictional teams played
from lobby to winner. All team names, pitches and answers are invented demo data, and the
public address is the placeholder `https://lions-den.example`.

The jury is Victoria Lyon (Market & Growth, The Growth Hunter), Maximilian Löwenstein
(Money & Margins, The Numbers Guy), Sophie Leone (Customers & Demand, The Market Realist)
and Dr. Alex Leander (Tech & AI, The AI Builder).

Viewports: projector captures use 1920x1080, team captures a 1440x900 laptop, vote captures
a 390x844 phone. The moderator dashboard and team details use 1440x1800 so the controls and
the full jury output stay readable; the export and past-run captures use 1440x900.
